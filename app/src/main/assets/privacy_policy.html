PaperPocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Scope and contact

PaperPocket is an Android app for importing images, scanning documents, creating image-based PDFs, and managing PDFs generated by the app. This policy covers package io.github.murtazaofficialskardu_cloud.paperpocket.

The PaperPocket developer can be contacted for privacy or support questions at murtazaofficialskardu@gmail.com.

Your images, scans, and PDFs

PaperPocket accesses images you select with Android's document picker and scan or camera results you choose to return to the app. It copies imported pages into private app storage so that drafts can be restored. Cropping, enhancement, rotation, PDF creation, and PDF viewing run locally.

Your draft pages, generated PDFs, document names, page counts, creation dates, file sizes, and editing/export preferences are stored on your device. PaperPocket does not automatically upload these documents or their contents to a publisher-operated server. The app has no accounts, advertising SDK, or publisher-operated analytics service.

Google document scanner

The optional scanner is supplied by Google ML Kit through Google Play services. Its components may need an internet download before first use. Document scanning runs on your device; ML Kit states that it does not send input images or scan results to Google servers. It may contact Google for updates and sends performance/use metrics under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google's ML Kit SDK collects technical metrics, including device/app information, identifiers, timing, configuration, event/error details, and input/output sizes, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google describes HTTPS transmission and no sharing of those listed metrics with third parties.

Google's disclosures and retention practices are available at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms, https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure, and https://policies.google.com/privacy. These SDK metrics are separate from your locally stored documents.

Permissions and other apps

PaperPocket does not request direct camera access or broad access to all photos or files. It uses Android's document picker, Google's scanner, or a system camera app. Internet access supports the Google scanner's downloadable components and SDK communications.

When you select Share, PaperPocket gives the app you choose temporary read access to that PDF through an Android content URI. When you select Save a copy, it writes the PDF to the location you choose. A cloud-backed picker, storage provider, camera app, or receiving app may handle files according to its own policies. PaperPocket does not control those providers.

Retention and deletion

Drafts and generated PDFs remain in private app storage until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Removing a draft page or clearing a draft removes the app's working copy without deleting the source photo you originally selected. A successful conversion clears the completed draft and keeps the generated PDF in My PDFs.

Deleting a PDF in My PDFs removes the app's library copy. Copies you exported or shared are separate and must be deleted in their destination app or storage provider. The app disables backup of its private documents. Export anything you want to keep before clearing app storage or uninstalling. Temporary working files may remain until the app or Android clears them.

PaperPocket does not offer a separate secure-erasure guarantee or an account deletion process, because the app has no user accounts. Google's SDK data retention is governed by Google's policies.

Security and support

Private files use Android's app storage isolation. Shared files use content URIs with read grants. PaperPocket does not add a separate password or encryption layer to its stored PDFs; device security and the permissions of destinations you choose still matter.

If you send a support message, the developer receives the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, or attachments, to respond to your request. Sending private document contents is not required to use the app. Your email provider's practices also apply.

Policy updates

This policy will be updated when PaperPocket's data handling or included SDKs change. An updated policy will show a revised date. The app includes an offline copy under Privacy & support.
