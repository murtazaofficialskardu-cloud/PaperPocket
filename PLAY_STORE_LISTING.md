## Full description

Turn photos, receipts, and everyday paperwork into PDFs with PaperPocket.

Import images through Android's document picker, or scan a document on supported Google Play services devices. Arrange your pages, adjust them, and create a PDF you can keep or share.

With PaperPocket you can:
• Import multiple images and build documents with up to 100 pages.
• Scan with automatic edge detection, straightening, and cleanup.
• Use a full-resolution camera photo when the Google scanner is unavailable.
• Reorder, rotate, remove, and perspective-crop pages.
• Choose original color, enhanced clarity, or black-and-white treatment.
• Export with A4, Letter, or image-proportional pages and optional margins.
• Choose Balanced for everyday documents, Compact for smaller files, or High for more detail.
• Reduce a generated PDF’s file size, keep the original, and see how much space the smaller copy saves.
• Follow page progress and cancel processing before the final save.
• Merge supported PaperPocket PDFs in a chosen order, or extract selected pages into a new PDF while retaining originals.
• Extract printed Latin/English text on your device, then copy it or share/save a TXT file.
• Find and rename PDFs generated by the app; move them to Trash, Undo or Restore, and confirm permanent deletion.
• View pages with zoom and pan, share a PDF, or save a copy to a location you choose.
• Continue a saved draft after reopening the app.

Your drafts and generated PDFs are stored in private app storage. No account, advertising, or purchasing is built into PaperPocket. Import, conversion, PDF viewing, and text extraction run locally. OCR uses a bundled Latin model with no first-use model download; extract up to 50 pages at a time. The Google scanner may need an initial component download and supported Google Play services. ML Kit technical metrics are handled by Google as described in the privacy policy.

High quality preserves detail available in the original photo; it cannot repair blur or create detail that was never captured. Text extraction returns separate text and can contain mistakes; review its output. Urdu, handwriting accuracy, and searchable PDF layers are not promised. Merge/page extraction supports the app's known image-PDF format and rejects unsupported PDFs. My PDFs lists documents generated by PaperPocket rather than every PDF on your device.

Export important PDFs before clearing app data or uninstalling. PaperPocket requires Android 8.0 or newer.

The full description is within the 4,000-character limit. The limits and required support email are documented in [Play's app setup guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en).
