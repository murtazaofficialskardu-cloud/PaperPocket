PaperPocket 1.1.0 beta - Android 8 or newer

Download/open the signed APK and choose Update. It shares the previous signing
certificate and package ID; do not uninstall first. PDFs/drafts should remain.
Export important PDFs before clearing data or uninstalling.

Default PDF quality is now Balanced. Compact produces smaller files; High keeps
more fine detail. A4/Letter/image dimensions and margins are under More options.
To reduce a saved PDF: My PDFs > its menu > Reduce file size > Save smaller copy.
The original remains. A copy is saved only when it is smaller; actual sizes appear.

Play Protect classification has not been cleared. See TESTING_AND_SECURITY.md and
the supplied owner-review draft. Do not disable device protection for testing.
A website or valid signature cannot guarantee removal of Google's warning.

AAB is the signed publishing bundle, not a directly installable app. Production
Play Store launch is postponed until phone feedback and release checks are done.
iOS and a hosted website have not been built.
