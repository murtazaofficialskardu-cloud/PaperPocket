PaperPocket 1.0.0 - Android 8 or newer

INSTALL: Download PaperPocket-1.0.0.apk on your Android phone, open it,
and allow installation from your browser/file manager if Android asks.
An AAB is for Play Console; it cannot be opened as an installed app.

USE: Import images or Scan document, arrange/crop/rotate pages, then
Create PDF. PDFs are saved in My PDFs; View, Share, or Save a copy.
Google's scanner requires supported Google Play services and may need
its first component download. System camera is available as fallback.

PLAY STORE: Use PaperPocket-1.0.0.aab on an internal test track first.
Finish the required real-phone checks in MANUAL_TESTS.md and your Console
account verification, store listing, Data safety, rating, and testing forms.
The store kit contains listing copy, artwork, public policy and test notes.
Public privacy policy:
https://github.com/murtazaofficialskardu-cloud/PaperPocket/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md

KEY BACKUP: Keep PaperPocket-PRIVATE-upload-key-backup.zip secure.
It includes the upload keystore and passwords needed for future releases.
Never add this private backup to GitHub or a public release.
Enroll in Play App Signing when making the first Console release.
A Play-signed app may need a fresh install if your sideloaded APK used
a different signing key; export any PDFs before uninstalling the app.
